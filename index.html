About RackRight

What we do

RackRight runs a 60,000 sq ft multi-client warehouse. We receive your goods, store them by SKU, pick and pack customer orders, and hand them to your courier the same day.

Who we serve

E-commerce sellers, D2C brands, small manufacturers and distributors who ship 100 to 5,000 orders a month and do not want to run their own warehouse.

Where we operate

Our facility is in Navi Mumbai, close to the JNPT port and the Mumbai-Pune expressway. We dispatch across Maharashtra, Gujarat and Goa, with pan-India courier partners beyond that.

Why customers stay

Stock accuracy above 99%, orders dispatched within 24 hours, and a dashboard you can open any time instead of waiting for an end-of-day report.

Compliance matters to us. We follow the warehouse registration rules published by the Warehousing Development and Regulatory Authority (WDRA), so you can issue electronic warehouse receipts against your stored goods.
